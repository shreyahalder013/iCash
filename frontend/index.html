Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2024

1. What is iCash? iCash is a prototype demonstration of a biometric banking interface. It is not a regulated financial institution. No real money is handled. Accounts, transactions, and balances exist in a demo database for testing purposes only.
2. Data We Collect When you register, we store: your name, Aadhaar last 4 digits (as a reference identifier, not verified with UIDAI), mobile number, email address, date of birth, and a facial descriptor vector for biometric authentication. Camera frames sent for liveness detection are processed in real-time and are not stored or recorded.
3. How We Use Your Data Data is used solely to authenticate you within this prototype and demonstrate banking functionality. We do not share your data with third parties. We do not sell data. This is a demo system.
4. Data Retention Data is stored on Render cloud infrastructure with PostgreSQL. You may delete your account at any time from Profile & Settings, which permanently removes all stored data.
5. Security Sessions use HTTP-only cookies. Passwords are hashed. Communication is over HTTPS/TLS. This is a prototype implementation and has not undergone a formal security audit.
6. Contact This is a prototype project. For questions, raise a grievance ticket within the application.
